## Detailed Block Analysis

### 1. Key Partners
* **180 Smallholder Farmers:** The core shareholder-members who supply crops across 6 staple and commercial horticulture categories (Tomatoes, Onions, Potatoes, Chillies, Capsicum, Pomegranates).
* **Village Collection Scouts (2 Entrepreneurs):** Local youth incentivized to verify harvest readiness, photograph produce accurately, and operate digital weighing scales.
* **Logistics & Aggregation Truckers:** Local fleet operators with 1.5–3.5 tonne light commercial vehicles (LCVs) for aggregated village pickups.
* **Institutional Buyer Network:** 35+ verified retail chains, B2B wholesale portals, and food processing corporations.
* **Digital Infrastructure Partners:** Meta WhatsApp Cloud API for automated catalogue broadcasts; ONDC Agri-Gateway for institutional discoverability.

### 2. Key Activities
* **Standardized Visual Verification:** Enforcing strict photography standards (color reference strip, cross-section cut, lighting, crated sample) to eliminate visual deceit.
* **Catalogue Curation:** Aggregating 40 weekly listings into a high-converting digital storefront refreshed every 72 hours.
* **Pre-Harvest Order Matching:** Securing binding purchase orders and advance deposits prior to physical harvest to eliminate perishable distress sales.
* **Consolidated Packing & Dispatch:** Sorting lots into standardized 20-kg HDPE ventilated crates with scannable batch tags.
* **Financial Clearing:** Managing escrow deposits and releasing automated payouts directly to farmer accounts within 24 hours of delivery.

### 3. Key Resources
* **Physical Infrastructure:** 3 village assembly sheds equipped with electronic platform scales, moisture meters, and optical grading tables.
* **Human Capital:** 1 Agronomist / Quality Lead, 2 Village Field Scouts, and 1 Digital Catalog Coordinator.
* **Digital Assets:** Progressive Web Catalogue application, automated WhatsApp Bot, farmer registration database, and buyer relationship CRM.
* **Logistics Assets:** Rotating pool of 600 stackable HDPE crates reducing fruit bruising.

### 4. Value Propositions
* **For Institutional Buyers:**
  - Guaranteed visual match with delivered consignment (zero "top-layer bait and switch").
  - 48 hours pre-harvest advance booking to ensure predictable supply chain planning.
  - Elimination of multiple mandi middleman margins, securing 8–12% lower procurement costs.
  - 24–36 hours longer shelf life due to farm-direct dispatch.
* **For Smallholder Farmers:**
  - Net price realization improvement of 18–26% over local APMC mandis.
  - 100% transparent digital electronic weighment (no illegal weighing deductions).
  - Elimination of transport risk; produce collected directly at village hub.

### 5. Customer Relationships
* **Institutional Accounts:** Dedicated relationship manager, monthly volume SLAs, custom invoicing, and guaranteed fulfillment buffers.
* **Mid-Sized Buyers:** Instant WhatsApp ordering bot, self-service web catalogue, real-time dispatch tracking.
* **Trust & Quality Assurance:** Clear 2-hour inspection window upon delivery with digital photographic dispute escalation.

### 6. Channels
* **Direct Digital Catalogue:** High-speed mobile-first web app accessible via web and mobile.
* **Bi-Weekly WhatsApp Broadcast:** Targeted push notifications sent to procurement managers at 7:00 AM on Monday and Thursday.
* **ONDC Agri-Exchange:** Integration with national digital commerce networks for algorithmic buyer matching.

### 7. Customer Segments
* **Primary (45%):** Modern Grocery Retail, Supermarket Chains, Quick-Commerce Hubs (Blinkit, Zepto, Reliance Fresh, More Retail).
* **Secondary (35%):** Hotel, Restaurant & Catering (HoReCa) clusters, corporate canteens, hospital dining networks.
* **Tertiary (20%):** Agro-processors (puree, chips, sauce manufacturers) and bulk terminal wholesalers.

### 8. Cost Structure
* **Field Personnel OPEX:** ₹36,000 / month (2 scouts @ ₹18k each).
* **Management & Digital Operations:** ₹28,000 / month (FPO lead).
* **Technology & Messaging:** ₹5,500 / month (Hosting, domains, WhatsApp API).
* **Hardware & Tool Maintenance:** ₹4,500 / month (Scales, moisture meters).
* **Crate Pool Amortization:** ₹12,000 / month (HDPE replacements & sanitization).
* **Contingency Spoilage Fund:** ₹16,632 / month (0.25% GMV buffer).
* **Total Monthly OPEX:** **₹1,02,632 / month**.

### 9. Revenue Streams
* **Facilitation Commission:** 3.5% on gross traded value = ₹2,32,848 / month.
* **Listing & Certification Fee:** ₹50 per lot = ₹8,400 / month.
* **Logistics Consolidation Surcharge:** 1.0% on consolidated transport = ₹66,528 / month.
* **Total Monthly Gross Revenue:** **₹3,07,776 / month**.
* **Monthly Net Retained Surplus:** **₹2,05,144 / month** (Annualized ₹24.61 Lakhs).
