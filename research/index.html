2. Core Problem vs. Market Opportunity Statement

The Core Problem (Traditional Mandi Friction): Smallholder farmers harvest perishable produce blindly without confirmed buyers. Upon transporting crops to physical APMC mandis in unventilated gunny bags, they face severe distress bargaining:

  • Arbitrary Visual Haircuts: Commission agents enforce 15% to 30% price discounts citing subjective quality flaws without objective tools.
  • Hidden Deductions (*Kata Chhoot*): Unofficial weighment cuts of 3% to 5% are deducted per bag for alleged dust, moisture, and handling.
  • Heavy Transit Spoilage: Rough handling and poor ventilation destroy 16% to 22% of produce before it reaches retail shelves.
  • Delayed Payment Terms: 15 to 45 day informal credit cycles drain working capital and trap farmers in exploitative local debt.

The Market Opportunity: Standardizing a weekly photo catalogue published 72 hours before harvest. By uploading 3 calibrated photos with physical reference strips, buyers lock Purchase Orders before harvesting begins. Produce is cut at dawn, packed directly into ventilated HDPE crates, and delivered to distribution centers within 24 hours of harvest.
