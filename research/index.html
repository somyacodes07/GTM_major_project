1. Core Problem & Opportunity Statements

The Core Problem: Smallholder farmers belonging to the 180-member FPO harvest without confirmed buyers. Arriving at APMC mandis uncommitted forces distress bargaining, where commission agents manipulate subjective grading and impose weighment cuts (*kata chhoot*), reducing farmer realization by 18% to 28%.

The Clear Opportunity: To build a weekly digital photo catalogue for 40 lots, enabling verified visual grading, scheduled farmgate collection, and direct digital linkage with urban B2B procurement partners (modern retail, HoReCa, food processors), capturing a 20%+ price premium for farmers while delivering fresh, traceable produce within 24 hours of harvest.

2. Information Gaps & Stakeholder Mapping

Farmer: Needs advance demand and price certainty before cutting crops. Solution: 72h advance catalogue listing and binding PO.

FPO QC Lead: Needs verified field yield and harvest timing to eliminate buyer disputes. Solution: Calibrated 3-photo upload with physical scale strip.

B2B Buyers: Need visual proof of maturity, confirmed quintals, and traceability. Solution: High-resolution photos, AGMARK Grade A/B/C cards, and origin batch tags.

3. Target Customers & Value Proposition Canvas

Segment 1: Modern Retail & Quick Commerce (45% Volume) — Require Grade A produce, daily replenishment, and +36h extended shelf-life.

Segment 2: HoReCa & Cloud Kitchens (35% Volume) — Require Grade A & B culinary staples, weekly contract price stability.

Segment 3: Agro-Processors (20% Volume) — Bulk absorption of Grade C produce for puree and sauces, ensuring zero farmgate dumping.

4. End-to-End Operating Protocol

Operates on a 5-stage SLA: (1) Farmer WhatsApp submission at T-72h, (2) Agronomist audit & Batch ID issuance at T-48h, (3) 7:00 AM catalogue broadcast at T-36h, (4) Pre-booking & synchronized dawn harvest at T-18h, (5) Consolidated LCV delivery and T+24h UPI settlement upon digital GRN sign-off.

5. Financial Calculations & Unit Economics

Weekly GMV: 40 lots $\times$ 18 Quintals $\times$ ₹2,200/Qtl = ₹15,84,000 / week.

Monthly GMV: ₹15,84,000 $\times$ 4.2 weeks = ₹66,52,800 (~₹66.5 Lakhs / month).

FPO Monthly Revenue: 3.5% commission (₹2,32,848) + ₹50 listing fees (₹8,400) + 1.0% logistics fee (₹66,528) = ₹3,07,776 / month.

FPO Monthly OPEX: 2 scouts + manager + API + crate fund = ₹1,02,632 / month.

Monthly Net Surplus: ₹3,07,776 - ₹1,02,632 = ₹2,05,144 / month (₹24.61 Lakhs / year).

Farmer Net Gain: Mandi payout is ₹1,751/Qtl vs. FPO payout of ₹2,202.75/Qtl $\rightarrow$ +₹451.75 / Qtl (+25.8% net cash increase), delivering ₹1.64 Crores in collective annual uplift.
