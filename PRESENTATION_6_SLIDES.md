### SLIDE 1: Problem Statement & Information Asymmetry
* **Slide Title:** Eliminating the Mandi Squeeze: Digital Transformation for 180 Smallholders
* **Sub-Header:** From Blind Harvests & Distress Selling to Pre-Harvest Discovery
* **Visual Elements:**
  - Split comparison graphic: Traditional Mandi (3-4 intermediaries, 25% price cut, 18% spoilage) vs. FPO Photo Catalogue (Direct farmgate link, advance booking, +22% realization).
  - Stat Callout: **180 Farmers | 40 Weekly Harvest Lots | ₹1.64 Cr Annual Member Uplift**.
* **Key Bullet Points:**
  - **The Farmer's Bottleneck:** Harvesting without confirmed buyers forces farmers into distress selling at local APMC mandis.
  - **The Buyer's Risk:** Modern retail and HoReCa buyers face high search costs, inconsistent sorting, and sight-unseen procurement risks.
  - **The Solution:** A lightweight weekly photo catalogue connecting farm availability with institutional demand 72 hours pre-harvest.
* **Speaker Script (2 Minutes):**
  > *"Good morning, evaluators. In Indian agriculture, smallholder farmers lose up to 30% of their produce value not because they cannot grow crops, but because they harvest blind. Our FPO represents 180 farmers who harvest around 40 distinct produce lots every single week. Today, they take uncommitted truckloads to physical mandis, where commission agents manipulate subjective grades and enforce price haircuts. Our project, the Farm Produce Photo Catalogue, changes this paradigm: we bring the produce to the buyer digitally 72 hours before the first sickle touches the field."*

### SLIDE 3: End-to-End Operating Protocol & Quality Assurance
* **Slide Title:** Standardizing Data: The 5-Stage Operating Flow
* **Sub-Header:** How We Guarantee >96% Catalogue Accuracy Across 40 Weekly Lots
* **Visual Elements:**
  - Flowchart: Farmer Submission (T-72h) ➔ FPO Scout Audit (T-48h) ➔ Catalogue Launch (T-36h) ➔ Buyer Booking (T-24h) ➔ Direct Delivery & UPI T+24h Settlement.
  - Graphic of the 3-Photo Standard (Canopy overview, Crate sample, Calibrated cross-section with reference scale).
* **Key Bullet Points:**
  - **Standardized Field Photos:** Color-calibrated strips and sizing scales eliminate deceptive photography.
  - **FPO Quality Lead Approval:** Dual-key verification before any lot goes live on the buyer web portal.
  - **Pre-Harvest Locking:** Binding digital purchase orders issued 24 hours prior to field harvesting.
* **Speaker Script (2 Minutes):**
  > *"The core problem statement explicitly challenges us: how do we keep information accurate for 40 lots every single week? We designed an error-proof operating flow. At T minus 72 hours, our village scout captures 3 standardized photographs using a calibrated physical reference scale. Our agronomist verifies the lot against AGMARK criteria before publishing. Buyers pre-book lots with advance deposits. Because produce is already sold before cutting, harvesting occurs at optimal dawn hours, packed into reusable ventilated HDPE crates, and delivered straight to the buyer's distribution center."*

### SLIDE 5: SWOT Analysis, Risk Mitigation & Buyer Engagement
* **Slide Title:** Risk Engineering & Proactive Buyer Engagement
* **Sub-Header:** Mitigating Visual Fraud, Transit Spoilage, and Price Volatility
* **Visual Elements:**
  - Risk & Mitigation Matrix (Visual discrepancy, weather shock, payment defaults, transit bruising, side-selling).
  - Two Growth Engines: (1) WhatsApp Cloud API Harvest Broadcast, (2) Farm-to-Shelf QR Traceability.
* **Key Bullet Points:**
  - **Visual Fraud Mitigation:** Mandatory FPO calibrated color strip in every photo; secondary inspection at hub.
  - **Perishable Protection:** Transition from gunny sacks to stackable HDPE crates reduces bruising by 70%.
  - **Buyer Engagement Engine:** Morning 7:00 AM WhatsApp broadcast with one-click RFQ pre-booking; dynamic QR code showing farm traceability on retail shelves.
* **Speaker Script (2 Minutes):**
  > *"Every agricultural venture faces operational risks. What happens if the delivered tomatoes don't match the photo? We mitigate this through calibrated physical reference strips and random 10% hub audits. How do we keep buyers engaged? First, a bi-weekly 7:00 AM WhatsApp interactive broadcast that lets procurement managers book lots in 90 seconds before morning trading. Second, a consumer-facing QR code on every crate that retail buyers can showcase in their stores as proof of farm-to-shelf traceability."*
