AGMARK Grading Benchmarks

  • Grade A: Maximum 2% superficial defect, uniform size diameter, optimal sugar/firmness balance.
  • Grade B: 2% to 6% cosmetic surface blemishes, completely sound internal pulp.
  • Grade C: Cosmetic variation / non-uniform sizes, 100% edible pulp for purees & processing.
